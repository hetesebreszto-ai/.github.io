# Local Clipper Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Local Clipper is a personal application operated locally
on its owner's computer.

## Google and YouTube access

The application uses Google authorization to read the
authorized YouTube channel's identity and upload video clips
to that channel.

OAuth credentials, channel information, upload records,
videos, and transcripts are stored locally on the
operator's computer. Videos and their metadata are sent
to YouTube when uploaded.

This website does not receive the application's OAuth
credentials, videos, transcripts, or upload records.
GitHub hosts this website and may process visitor information
under its own privacy statement.

## Control and deletion

The operator can stop the application, revoke its access
through Google Account settings, delete local application
data, and delete uploaded videos through YouTube Studio.
